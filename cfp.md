# Call for Papers

The call for papers is open! Submissions can be made through the [HotCRP submission site](https://icse2027-trustedsoftwair.hotcrp.com/).  

The TrustedSoftwAIr workshop invites papers and discussions on producing, evaluating, and verifying software whose development is assisted or automated by AI.

<hr>

### Topics of Interest

- AI-driven proof engineering
- Specification formalization
- Neuro-symbolic and agentic approaches
- Language and theorem prover design
- AI-augmented software analysis and testing
- Trustworthy and secure code synthesis
- Explainability and correctness of AI tools
- Human-AI collaboration
- Human trust and perception of AI-written code
- Cross-pollination with AI for Mathematics
- Data and benchmark curation
- Evaluation metrics
- Model architectures and scale

### Submission Requirements

We invite three types of submissions:

1. **Research papers** (up to 8 pages, including references) presenting new techniques, tools, benchmarks, or empirical studies on any of the workshop's topics of interest.

2. **Vision and early-idea extended abstracts** (up to 2 pages, excluding references). Short submissions describing new ideas, work in progress, or positions on where the field should go, intended to spark discussion at the workshop.

3. **Presentations of already published work.** Submit an abstract of a previously published paper relevant to TrustedSoftwAIr, to present this work at the workshop to increase the work's visibility in the community. These submissions will not be published in the TrustedSoftwAIr proceedings.

Manuscripts must be written in English and submitted as PDF files through the workshop’s [HotCRP site](https://icse2027-trustedsoftwair.hotcrp.com/).

Research paper and extended abstract submissions must conform to the IEEE conference proceedings template, specified in the [IEEE Conference Proceedings Formatting Guidelines](https://www.ieee.org/conferences/publishing/templates.html) (title in 24pt font and full text in 10pt type, LaTeX users must use `\documentclass[10pt,conference]{IEEEtran}` without including the `compsoc` or `compsocconf` options). These formatting guidelines do not apply to submissions for presentations of already published work.

TrustedSoftwAIr'27 will use a single-blind review process, so every submission must include the authors' names.

Changes to the author list subsequent to submission will *not* be allowed.

If a submission is accepted, at least one author of the paper is required to register for TrustedSoftwAIr'27 and present the paper in person. Accepted papers will be displayed on the TrustedSoftwAIr webpage.
