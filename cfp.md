# Call for Papers

The call for papers is open! Submissions can be made through the [submission link](https://icse2027-trustedsoftwair.hotcrp.com/).  

The TrustedSoftwAIr workshop invites papers and discussions on producing, evaluating, and verifying software whose development is assisted or automated by AI.

<hr>

### Topics of Interest

- AI-driven proof engineering
- Specification formalization
- Neuro-symbolic and agentic approaches
- Language and theorem prover design
- AI-augmented software analysis and testing
- Trustworthy and secure code synthesis
- Explainability and correctness of AI tools
- Human-AI collaboration
- Human trust and perception of AI-written code
- Cross-pollination with AI for Mathematics
- Data and benchmark curation
- Evaluation metrics
- Model architectures and scale

### Submission Requirements

We invite three types of submissions:

1. **Research papers** (up to 8 pages, including references). Full papers presenting research results on any of the workshop's topics of interest, such as new techniques, tools, benchmarks, or empirical studies.

2. **Vision and early-idea extended abstracts** (up to 2 pages, excluding references). Short submissions describing new ideas, work in progress, or positions on where the field should go, intended to spark discussion at the workshop.

3. **Presentations of already published work** (non-archival). Proposals to present work that has been recently published or is under submission to another venue, giving it exposure to the workshop's cross-disciplinary audience. Please mark such papers upon submission.

Details on formatting will be announced when ICSE 2027 workshop guidelines are available.

All accepted papers will be presented at the workshop. Accepted papers will be displayed on the TrustedSoftwAIr homepage and are considered non-archival.
